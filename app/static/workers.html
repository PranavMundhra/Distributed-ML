Checking controls…
  1. Send six requests and watch the worker IDs alternate.
  2. Pause one worker. Its heartbeats stop and new inference is rejected.
  3. Wait for it to become unavailable, then send six requests to the survivor.
  4. Resume the paused worker. Its next heartbeat restores eligibility.

Pause is a simulated service failure: the process stays running so it can resume. A real process crash can also be demonstrated by stopping a worker in its terminal. Requests already running may finish; failed predictions are not automatically retried.
